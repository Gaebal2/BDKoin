# BDKoin brand assets

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project, guided by the user's wallet reference image.

- Hero: `pwa/images/bdkoin-brand.png`
- Installed app PNG: `pwa/icons/bdkoin-app.png`
- Small vector icon: `pwa/icons/icon.svg` and `pwa/images/bdk-token-icon.svg`

The hero and app icon are recreations, not extracted original brand source files. The vector icon is a code-native adaptation for crisp small-size rendering.

## Hero prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: BDKoin wallet hero branding. Input image is the user-provided wallet design reference. Reproduce only its upper branding as a clean high-resolution square asset on pure black: the circular fine gold ring containing the exact angular interlocking B / D monogram, brushed beveled metallic gold, subtle radial gold light streaks behind, and below it the exact stylized word "BDKoin" with the small tagline "ART FOR PEACE & CHILDREN". Preserve the emblem and wordmark shapes, gold-on-black luxury style, and proportions as closely as possible. Fill central 85 percent of square with emblem above wordmark. No balance card, no navigation, no other UI, no extra text. Outer edges pure black for seamless embedding in black webpage. Save image output to file.

## App icon prompt

Square app icon, 1024x1024. A polished metallic champagne-gold circular thin ring enclosing an angular intertwined capital B and D monogram, BDKoin cryptocurrency brand. Pure flat opaque pitch-black #000000 background. This is clean minimal vector-like geometric gold logo artwork with a subtle metal bevel ONLY INSIDE the emblem strokes. Entire emblem within central 70% of square. Absolutely NO glow, NO rays, NO flames, NO red, NO yellow halo, NO colored pixels outside the sharp emblem edges, NO text, NO wordmark, NO shadows. Clean black negative spaces inside the letters. Rounded gold B strokes are angular polygonal chevrons like the reference brand. The two upright letter stems extend above and below the body with diagonal cuts.
